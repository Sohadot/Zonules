## Status

This standard governs the `/acquire/` surface and any communication that surrounds it. It is subordinate to `ASSET_THESIS.md`. It exists because the acquisition surface is the one place where a reference asset can quietly destroy its own authority — by sounding like a domain listing. This standard forbids that.

The governing principle is drawn directly from the asset's own thesis:

> A seeing system is trusted because of the hidden structure that holds its focus. An asset is acquired because of the hidden structure that holds its value. The acquisition surface must show the structure, not beg for the sale.

---

## The Evidence Posture

The asset does not ask to be bought. It shows what has been built, what can be verified, and what remains conceptual, so that a strategic buyer can reach their own conclusion from evidence, not from pressure.

The surface may explain what rebuilding the integrated package would involve (terminology design, source verification, classification architecture, reference relationships, governance, implementation, multilingual coordination). It must present that as a matter of record, never as proof of commercial value or as a claim that the package cannot be recreated.

*(v0.1 called this the "Inevitability Posture". The public surface now states its case through verifiable structure; the underlying principle — authority, not pressure — is unchanged.)*

---

## What the Surface Must Do

1. State plainly that Zonules.com is a developed, governed reference framework — not a domain listing. Internal terms such as "Category Intelligence Factory" remain repository language and are not used as public claims.
2. Show the structure that constitutes the asset: the governing thesis, the three-layer reference architecture, the Focus Integrity Ontology and Standard, the Focus Integrity Engine, the governance and decision record, the multilingual architecture, and the static-first security posture.
3. Separate what is implemented and verifiable (computed from the registries at build time) from what is conceptual or unvalidated.
4. Name buyer *categories*, never individual buyers, and keep three things distinct: fit of the anatomical name, potential utility of the framework, and evidence of demand (none is claimed).
5. State what is not included: established scientific terminology, public concepts, cited third-party works, and any unverified rights.
6. Include a short **Secure transaction** section: an independent escrow service may be used by mutual agreement; it is a mechanism, not proof of ownership or a substitute for a transfer agreement. No provider is named and no arrangement is implied.
7. Offer one calm, institutional contact path: `agent@sohadot.com`.
8. Remain readable as reference-grade material — the same voice as every other surface.

---

## Absolute Prohibitions (enforced by the quality gate)

The acquisition surface must never contain:

- Price figures, currency symbols, or numeric valuations
- Auction, bidding, "make an offer", "buy now", or marketplace framing
- Urgency or scarcity language: "act now", "limited time", "hurry", "don't miss", "won't last", countdowns
- Public naming of any specific prospective buyer or company as a target
- Discount, deal, or sale framing
- Any claim that the asset *needs* to be sold
- Unsupported claims: "indispensable", "impossible to recreate", "exclusive ownership of the category", "scientifically validated", "guaranteed", or "sovereign" / "Category Intelligence Factory" as public descriptors
- Checkout, payment integration, or a published asking price
- Fabricated buyer interest, traffic, revenue, partnerships, market size, or valuation

The surface must also carry the Secure Transaction (escrow) section and the `mailto:` contact path.

These are checked automatically. A single violation blocks publication of the surface (Quality Gate item 15).

## Voice

Calm. Institutional. Evidence-based. Strategically restrained. The acquisition surface may be confident. It may not be eager. It may state value. It may not chase it.

> The asset attracts strategic inquiry through authority. It does not generate artificial urgency.

---

## Governing Sentences

> **Show the structure, not the price.**

> **Name the category, never the buyer.**

> **The asset must not need the acquisition to succeed.**
